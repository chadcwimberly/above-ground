Support

If you're in crisis or might hurt yourself, please reach out now. In the app, tap "Need more help?" at the top of any screen for crisis lines where you are. You can also call your local emergency number, or find a free helpline at findahelpline.com. In the US, call or text 988.

Contact us

Questions, ideas, or something not working? Email hello@aboveground.app. It helps to include your device and the version shown at the bottom of Settings.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Above Ground signs you in anonymously the first time you open it. There's no email or password.

How do I use Above Ground on more than one device?

On the device you already use, open Settings → Show my sync code. On the other device, open Settings → I have a sync code, then type the 12 words or scan the QR code. Linked devices share settings and history. Keep the words private: anyone with them can see and change your synced settings.

I lost my sync code.

Open Settings → Show my sync code on any linked device to see it again. If no linked device is left, the code can't be recovered, because only your devices know the words. Make a new one from Settings.

How do I download or delete my data?

Settings → Download or delete my data. Deleting is immediate. See the privacy policy for exactly what's stored.

Why does it suggest some exercises more often?

Above Ground learns from your thumbs up and down and from which exercises you skip, then leans toward the ones that suit you. New exercises still come up so you can try them.

What do contributions pay for?

Above Ground was made to help keep our most vulnerable loved ones here with us – above ground. It's free, with no ads, and always will be. Contributions go toward hosting and keeping the app free for everyone. They're optional and unlock nothing. Find them in Settings → Support Above Ground.

Is this medical advice?

No. The exercises draw on research about coping skills, and each one explains why it works, but Above Ground isn't a replacement for professional care.
