Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Above Ground is a free app with short, research-based exercises for anxiety, low mood and grounding. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never asks for your name or email. This page explains the little it does store, why, and how to download or delete it. It covers the website at aboveground.app and the iPhone and Android apps.

What we store

When you first open Above Ground, it signs you in with an anonymous ID: a random code that isn't linked to your name, email, phone number or any other account. Under that ID we store:

  • A device record: a random friendly name (like "Kind Cookie Pony"), when it was created and when it was last used.
  • How you use the exercises: which exercises, affirmations and closing messages were shown, when you moved forward or back or asked for a different one, your thumbs up and down, and completed sessions, each with a time and a random session number. We use this only to choose exercises that suit you better.
  • Your settings, so they can follow you to your other devices: theme, font, vibration, reminder time and days, and the country used for crisis lines.
  • If you turn on sync: a scrambled fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash) of your 12-word sync code, so another device can link with it, plus a record of recent link attempts to limit guessing. The words themselves stay on your devices.
  • Contribution message state: when the message inviting contributions was last shown, and whether you chose "Don't show again".

What we don't collect

  • Anything you type into an exercise. It stays on your device and is cleared when you move on.
  • Your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The crisis-line country is a guess from your device's language and region settings, which you can change.
  • Advertising IDs, analytics or tracking of any kind. We don't sell or share data for advertising.

Device features (apps only)

  • Reminders are scheduled on your device. They're off unless you turn them on, and no message server is involved.
  • Apple Health and Health Connect: if you turn on "Save mindful minutes", the app writes each completed session to your device's health app as a mindful session. It doesn't read any health data and never sends health data to us. Health data is never used for advertising.
  • Camera: used only when you choose to scan a sync QR code. Images are processed on your device and never saved or sent anywhere.

Contributions

Contributions are optional and unlock nothing. In the apps, Apple or Google handles the payment, and we only receive their sales reports. On the website, Stripe handles the payment. Stripe shares the details you give it (such as your email and the amount) with us so we can keep records and answer questions. We never see your card details. Each provider's own privacy policy covers its part.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase hosts the database and the anonymous sign-in.
  • Netlify hosts the website.
  • Stripe, Apple and Google handle contributions.

Like any website, these services see your IP address when your device connects, and may keep it briefly in their logs for security. Your data may be processed in the United States.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Settings → Download or delete my data → Download my data gives you everything stored under your profile as a file.
  • Delete your data: in the same place, delete this device's data, or everything on all linked devices. It's deleted from the database right away, including the anonymous ID, and the app starts fresh. Copies in routine backups expire on their own shortly after.
  • You can also email us to ask for a copy or deletion.

We keep your data until you delete it. These choices apply wherever you live, including your rights under laws like the GDPR and the CCPA.

Children

Above Ground isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Not medical care

Above Ground supports everyday moments. It isn't a replacement for professional care. If you're in crisis, use "Need more help?" in the app, call your local emergency number, or find a free helpline at findahelpline.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what we collect, we'll say so in the app first.
